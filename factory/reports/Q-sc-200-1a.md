## Notes, doubts, deviations
- The WebFetch budget ran out once during the session. Because of that, "Manage automation file uploads" (content analysis) and the Asset rule management page weren't fetched, and no question relies on them. Asset rule management is used only for what the custom data collection pages say about it: dynamic tags are created there.
- Course chapter 11 (section rules-settings) says an indicator "usually takes effect within 15 to 30 minutes, up to 2 hours". On Learn, that timing is for **file** indicators only. The IP/URL/domain page says blocks "can take up to 48 hours", and usually take effect within two hours. The course owner may want to separate the two.
- The Defender for Endpoint "automated investigations" page still describes "Initiate Automated Investigation" in its body. Its September 1, 2026 notice, and the Action center and automation levels pages, say AIR can no longer be triggered manually. `sc200-1a-air-expansion` follows the notice: a full antivirus scan for on-demand investigations.
- `sc200-1a-asr-cloud-rules`: Learn lists three rules that need cloud-delivered protection: prevalence/age/trusted list, advanced ransomware protection, and obfuscated scripts. Only the first two are offered as options, so the multi keeps exactly two correct answers.
- In French, the portal blade names stay in English (Actions and submissions > Action center, Workspace scope, Define rule scope). Device tags are written « étiquettes dynamiques » to match the SC-200 course chapters.
