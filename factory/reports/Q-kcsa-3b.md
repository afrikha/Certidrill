- `kcsa-3b-anon-flag-and-file`: the doc says only that `--anonymous-auth` "cannot be set" with the file's `anonymous` field. The answer avoids naming the exact failure (startup error).
- `kcsa-3b-cani-group-check`: relies on "Impersonate-Group requires Impersonate-User" (User Impersonation page). The kubectl error text for `--as-group` without `--as` was not checked.
- `kcsa-3b-cani-api` distractor: `SelfSubjectReview` = `kubectl auth whoami` comes from the course chapter and report (verified there), not re-fetched by me.
- `kcsa-3b-rbac-namespace-patch`: follows the RBAC good-practices wording (patch on the namespace through a namespaced RoleBinding). The exact RBAC scoping of a namespaced Role on the `namespaces` resource is not detailed further.
- `kcsa-3b-secret-missing-required` option c: "the Deployment is accepted even if the Secret doesn't exist" is general knowledge. The page only says that the kubelet retries.
- `kcsa-3b-ns-kube-public`: "readable by all clients" is a documented convention, so the stem and answer say "By convention".
- Env vars vs volumes: the Secret good-practices page doesn't prefer either. The Security Checklist does (volumes), so that is the page cited.
