- Soft delete is now "secure by default" (cannot be disabled in GA regions): no question relies on disabling soft delete.
- The MUA concept page does not mention a region requirement for the Resource Guard (the existing series sr2 relies on one); `bk-mua-setup` does not use regions.
- `iac-stack-excluded`: requirement worded "directly", since an Owner could in principle change the stack's deny settings itself.
