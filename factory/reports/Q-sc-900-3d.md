## Doubts and choices to review

- **3.4.7 Microsoft Threat Intelligence is moving.** The training unit is still titled "Describe Microsoft Defender Threat Intelligence" and teaches Threat analytics, Intel profiles, Intel explorer and Projects; the docs (updated 2026-09-23) describe a new experience with two pages, **Overview** and **Intel explorer**, where threat analytics reports live. The questions rely only on what both describe: threat analytics reports (Microsoft researchers, your own data, defensive guidance), the dashboard categories (latest, high-impact, highest exposure), Intel explorer search, indicators of compromise, the Threat intelligence node, and the convergence of the retired standalone Defender TI into Defender XDR and Sentinel. **Intel profiles and Intel projects are not tested** (absent from the new docs pages), although course chapter 34 teaches them.
- `ti-standalone-converged` depends on the August 1, 2026 retirement (training unit note and Intel explorer page). The date appears only in the explanation.
- `ti-sentences-complete`: "search by keyword, indicator or CVE ID" is the training unit's wording; the new Intel explorer page says keyword or value plus a threat intelligence type (vulnerabilities included).
- **Defender for Identity scope.** The training unit and the overview page now say that it also monitors Microsoft Entra ID signals and other identity providers (Okta). No question tests this, and no question says that it is limited to on-premises Active Directory; `mdi-onprem-ad-scenario` decides on "must monitor the domain controllers". Course chapter 34 could mention the extension.
- **Vulnerability management location in the portal** (`mdvm-sentences-complete`, `portal-areas-match`): the training unit puts it under Exposure management; the docs say this applies to preview customers (existing customers still see their previous navigation, and the 2024 portal overview page lists it under Endpoints). "Endpoints" is never offered as a wrong option.
- `mdvm-scores-direction`: the exposure score model is being replaced (docs, rollout in progress) and the page warns that the score is not guaranteed to move monotonically after a remediation; the stem asks how the scores are "expected to change".
- `mdvm-remediation-request` and its explanation name Microsoft Intune as the place where the remediation task is created (training unit and docs); Intune itself is not tested.
- `portal-experience-yn` (navigation pane customization is per user) and `portal-action-center` come from the training unit; they sit at the upper edge of SC-900 detail.
- **Not tested on purpose**: Security Copilot integrations and agents (no objective), Defender Experts for XDR, exposure score ranges, advanced hunting quotas, Secure Score point values, Defender for Identity deployment tools.
