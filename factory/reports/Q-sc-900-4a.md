## Points to review

- **French terminology not verified on fr-fr pages** (the hourly fetch limit was reached before the fr-fr pages could be
  read). The questions follow the terms of course chapters 41, 42 and 43. Terms that the chapters don't contain carry the
  English name in parentheses: « Mon historique de téléchargement (My Download History) », « Tous les documents (All
  Documents) », « Centre de connaissances (Knowledge Center) », « élément principal (primary element) », « élément de
  prise en charge (supporting element) », « proximité (proximity) », « niveau de confiance (confidence level) »,
  « amorçage » (seeding), and the three sections of the Purview Solutions page.
- **4.2.1 `sc900-4a-portal-sections-match`.** Uses Data Loss Prevention and Insider Risk Management (Data Security),
  eDiscovery (Risk & Compliance) and Unified Catalog (Data Governance). The training unit names the catalog "Data Catalog";
  the portal page names it "Unified Catalog". Data Lifecycle Management and Audit are left out because Learn sources
  disagree on their section.
- **4.2.1 `sc900-4a-portal-home-yn`.** The list of related portals (Defender, Entra, Fabric, Priva, Service Trust) comes
  from the Purview portal page; portal menus change often.
- **4.2.3.** Three questions rely on the 27/9/3/1 point table and on the preventative/detective/corrective definitions of
  the scoring page (`sc900-4a-score-points-risk`, `sc900-4a-score-corrective-action`, `sc900-4a-score-actions-hotarea`).
  The training unit only says that points depend on risk: this may be more detail than the real exam asks.
- **4.3.2 `sc900-4a-expl-content-viewer-role`** (hard) tests the two Content explorer role groups; the docs page is now
  titled "Content Explorer (classic)". `sc900-4a-expl-details-yn` uses the 30-day limit of Activity explorer.
- **4.3.2.** The content explorer page gives the path Data Lifecycle Management > Explorers, the training unit says
  Information Protection: no question depends on the navigation path.
- **4.1.2.** Questions follow the four principles of the current Learn unit (as the course does), not the older list of six.
- Reference titles use the page title without the " | Microsoft Learn" / " - Training" suffix.
