## Facts I used without seeing them on a fetched page (well established, but flag them for review)

- `netdom query fsmo` lists the FSMO role holders (az802-1a-fsmo-find-pdc).
- `Uninstall-ADDSDomainController -RemoveDnsDelegation` exists. It is used only as a distractor in az802-1a-dc-remove-last-dc.

- A VM picks up changed VNet DNS servers after a restart or renewal (az802-1a-azdc-spoke-dns).
- Child-domain PDC emulators sync time from the forest root through the domain hierarchy (az802-1a-fsmo-time-source).
- KCC least-cost path selection across bridged site links (az802-1a-site-link-cost).
- `netdom trust /add` between domains of two forests creates an external trust. This is inferred from the Netdom page, which says netdom cannot create a forest trust between AD DS forests (az802-1a-trust-direction s3).
