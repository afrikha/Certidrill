## Sources and deviations
- "Lift and shift / lift-shift-migrate / lift-shift-migrate to HANA" are not defined verbatim on current Learn pages. Questions map them like chapter 11 does: homogeneous copy (same OS family, DBMS and byte order) / heterogeneous copy with SWPM (OS, DBMS or endianness change to a non-HANA target) / DMO to HANA. Grounded on the training modules explore-migration-options (units 4–8) and migrate-very-large-databases-to-azure (units 2, 5, 6).
- The CAF SAP pages (identity and access management, landing zone accelerator, migrate) are marked deprecated (removal 30 Oct 2026) and were not used as references. Kerberos/SNC facts come from the training unit explore-identity-services/9; IAS/BTP facts from the Entra scenario page "Using Microsoft Entra ID to secure access to SAP platforms and applications".
- Azure Migrate "discover SAP systems (preview)" now redirects to the Azure Migrate hub: not used.
