## Points to review

- **3.4.7 Microsoft Threat Intelligence.** The outline says "Microsoft Threat Intelligence"; the Learn unit is still titled
  "Describe Microsoft Defender Threat Intelligence" and announces the retirement of the standalone Defender TI product on
  2026-08-01 (capabilities converged into Defender XDR and Sentinel). The chapter teaches the four items of the Learn unit
  (threat analytics, intel profiles, intel explorer, intel projects). The docs describe a newer portal experience
  (Overview + Intel explorer pages, with renamed types) that replaces Threat analytics and Intel management: not taught, to
  re-check when the Learn unit is updated.
- **4.1.2 Privacy principles.** The current Learn unit lists four principles (you control your data; know where your data is
  located; your data is secured at rest and in transit; Microsoft defends your data). The older list of six principles
  (control, transparency, security, strong legal protections, no content-based targeting, benefits to you) is no longer on
  the pages fetched and was left out. Questions should follow the four.
- **4.3.2 Content explorer.** The docs now title the page "Content Explorer (classic)" and name the newer experience
  "Data explorer" (Information Protection > Explorers). The chapter keeps the outline's name and mentions Data explorer in
  one sentence. The role-group names are kept in English in French (fr-fr spelling not verified).
- **4.2.1 Purview portal.** Learn sources disagree on which solution sits in which portal category (the training unit puts
  Data Lifecycle Management under Data Governance; the learning path treats it as a data compliance solution). The chapter
  groups solutions by family "as seen in this course", not as a copy of the portal menu.
- **3.2.2 Regulatory compliance.** The Defender CSPM plan table marks "Regulatory compliance assessments" as Defender CSPM
  only, while the training unit says Foundational CSPM includes MCSB compliance. The chapter follows both: MCSB in
  Foundational, other regulatory standards in the paid plan.
- **3.3.2 Sentinel.** UEBA is not taught (absent from the pages fetched). Azure portal support ends after 2027-03-31
  (overview page). The data lake is mentioned in one sentence; graph and MCP server (present in the Learn unit) are not.
- **3.4.2 Safe Links "at the time of the click" and Safe Attachments "before delivery"** come from product knowledge of
  the Defender for Office 365 docs; the pages fetched describe the features without those exact words.
- **4.2.3** The 27/9/3/1 points table is from the Compliance Manager scoring page; it may be more detail than the exam
  asks at this level.

  Office 365, Defender pour point de terminaison, Defender pour les applications cloud, Defender pour Identity, Gestion des
