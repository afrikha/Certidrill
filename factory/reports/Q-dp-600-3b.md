## Deviations and doubts

- The size limit of a small-format model on a capacity is not stated clearly on the large models page (the fetched summary said "default 1 GB"; the course says 10 GB). The questions avoid relying on it: every scenario uses sizes above 10 GB, or a Pro workspace (1 GB, verified).
- Direct Lake overview says Direct Lake on SQL can be "extended in Power BI Desktop with Import, DirectQuery, or Dual tables", but the composite models page says Direct Lake on SQL can't join any composite model. No question tests Direct Lake on SQL in composite models.
- dp600-3b-perf-direct-lake-agg: the correct answer (a summary Delta table built upstream) is a design inference. What Learn states is that Direct Lake does not support user-defined aggregations, hybrid tables or model partitions.
- The automatic aggregations page lists Premium per capacity, PPU and Embedded, not Fabric F SKUs, so dp600-3b-perf-auto-agg uses a PPU workspace.
- dp600-3b-lsm-on-demand and dp600-3b-perf-pa-steps test a decision that is close to case-study questions dp600-cs1b-large-format and dp600-cs4a-trends-performance, but they use another format and another angle (on-demand load; the order of the steps).
