## Deviations and doubts

- **The AI-901 learning path has no responsible AI unit.** The six principles are written from the unit "Identify guiding principles for responsible AI" and the two overview pages above, as the course chapter 11 did.
- **Questions that go beyond pure principle identification** (to give 15 distinct questions per objective, in line with chapter 11): Foundry default guardrails and evaluators (`fair-yn-guardrail-category`, `rel-yn-default-guardrails`, `rel-dd-block-or-measure`, `rel-two-measures-agent`), secrets / Key Vault / managed identity / RBAC (`priv-limit-access-rbac`, `priv-yn-secrets`, `priv-dd-key-storage`), PII detection (`priv-redact-chat-text`, `priv-order-call-transcripts`), speech recognition vs synthesis (`incl-dd-speech-capabilities`). The real exam may stay at the level of the principles; drop these first if the bank must be stricter on scope.
- **Principle attribution follows Microsoft Learn where two principles could be argued**: "seek human input / hand over to a human" is tested under reliability and safety (`rel-human-input`, accountability is not among the options); "tell customers how data is collected, used and stored" and "controls over data use" are tested under privacy and security (`priv-collection-obligation`, `priv-customer-controls`, `priv-yn-insurance-claims`). Scenarios that Learn does not settle were avoided (cyberattacks, quality of service by demographic group, language coverage).
- `priv-redact-chat-text`: the distractor explanations for named entity recognition and key phrase extraction are general descriptions; their Learn pages were not fetched.
- `priv-dd-key-storage`: "no other credential has to be stored with the application" (managed identity) is general Azure knowledge; the fetched unit only says the secret is retrieved at runtime with a managed identity.
- `fair-approval-by-group`: the figures are invented for the scenario; "compare results across sensitive groups, not overall accuracy alone" comes from the fairness assessment description on the Azure Machine Learning page and chapter 11.
- **French**: principle names as in the study guide and chapter 11 (impartialité, inclusion, responsabilité; the fr-fr unit says « équité »). « sous-titres en temps réel » (the fr-fr unit says « légendes »), « personnes en situation de handicap » (fr-fr: « personnes handicapées »), « invites contradictoires » for adversarial prompts and « évaluateurs de risque et de sécurité » were not checked on a fr-fr page. Guardrail category names and thresholds stay in English (Hate and fairness, Violence, Medium, Low), with « Haine et impartialité » given once as on the fr-fr page. Inclusive design principles: my own translation followed by the English name in parentheses, because the fr-fr unit's wording is a faulty machine translation (« Résolvez-en un, étendez-en plusieurs »).
- Reference titles are the H1 of the pages as reported by the fetch tool; the browser tab title of "Default Guardrail policies for Microsoft Foundry" is "Default safety policies in Microsoft Foundry".
