## Recent facts verified on learn.microsoft.com (2026-11-17) worth a reviewer's eye

- Sentinel in the Azure portal unsupported after March 31, 2027 (several pages).
- DLP: built-in alert tuning rule (early October 2026) turns DLP signals into behaviors (`BehaviorInfo`, `BehaviorEntities`); disable under Settings > Microsoft Defender XDR > Alert tuning.
- Incident cases (preview, Cases > Incident) are "the recommended experience"; resolving a case doesn't resolve related alerts (legacy page: resolving resolves linked alerts). Case management: Sentinel workspace required, IDs from 1000, statuses New/Open/Closed, RBAC table.
- Blast radius analysis replaces attack path analysis; needs Sentinel data lake onboarding and Exposure management (read); 8 paths, up to 7 hops (5 cloud, 5 on-prem, 3 hybrid).
- Phishing Triage Agent prerequisites, Entra Agent ID, Auto-Resolve rule must be disabled; Security Alert Triage Agent extension (preview).
- Custom detections: frequencies/lookbacks, Continuous table list and restrictions, 150 alerts per run, actions and required columns, management actions (Run, Edit, Modify query, Turn off, Delete, Include/exclude from correlation).
- NRT: 50 rules per customer, 12-hour ingestion delay limit, 30 single-event alerts, multiple tables allowed. Scheduled: 10 entity types, suppression 24 h, 150 alerts per incident, no reopen in Defender portal; alert-trigger playbooks deprecated March 2026.
- MITRE page aligned to ATT&CK v18. Anomalies: duplicate once, " - Customized", FLGT; DGA anomalies discontinued March 8, 2026; training period 7–21 days.
- TI: legacy `ThreatIntelligenceIndicator` stopped July 31, 2025; ingestion rules 15 min, connectors only.
- MDCA: dynamic threat detection model since June 2025 (renamed detections); file policies retire January 6, 2027.
- MDI remediation actions and "SOC Identity Responder" role exist on the remediation page (not used in the chapter).
