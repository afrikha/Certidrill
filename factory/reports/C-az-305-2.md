## Points for the reviewer

- **Site Recovery RTO.** "RTO of up to one hour, no SLA for RPO" is quoted from the zone-to-zone article ("same as the SLA for Site Recovery overall"). The reliability article gives no number. Worth a second look against the SLA document itself.
- **Site Recovery retention.** "Recovery points kept 15 days at most" was read on the VMware (modernized) article; the Azure-to-Azure article states only the 1-day default.
- **Enhanced VM backup policy.** Instant-restore retention "1 to 30 days, default 7" (enhanced-policy article and "key capabilities" of the instant-restore article). The cost section of the instant-restore article says "5 to 20 days": Learn contradicts itself; I kept 1–30.
- **Backup vault soft delete.** "New Recovery Services vaults can't disable it" (secure by default, GA). For Backup vaults the always-on behaviour is GA in three regions only and preview elsewhere, so the chapter limits the claim to Recovery Services vaults.
- **Vaulted blob backup.** Schedule options and maximum retention were not readable on the fetched pages (tabbed content); the chapter gives no number for them. The 360-day maximum of operational backup is verified.
- **Smart tier.** Present on the access-tiers page (moves data between hot, cool and cold); the page does not say GA or preview, and neither does the chapter.
- **Synapse Link for Azure Cosmos DB.** Learn now says "no longer supported for new projects", replaced by Cosmos DB mirroring in Fabric. Chapter 23 gives both (existing designs / new projects), because exam items written before the change may still name Synapse Link.
- **Microsoft Fabric.** Not in the Learn module "Design data integration" (which covers Data Factory, Data Lake, Databricks, Synapse, hot/warm/cold paths, Stream Analytics), but Learn's Data Factory and Synapse pages now recommend Fabric for new work. Chapter 23 keeps the module's services as the core and adds one paragraph and two table rows on Fabric.
- **Synapse Data Explorer pool** is still labelled preview on the Synapse overview; marked so in the table.
- **Azure SQL Hybrid Benefit.** Not offered for new Hyperscale databases (existing provisioned ones until December 2026); the chapter recommends it for General Purpose and Business Critical only.
- **Stated from stable knowledge rather than from a page fetched today:** Azure Storage data is always encrypted at rest and customer-managed keys live in Key Vault; Data Factory trigger types (schedule, tumbling window, event); the four Azure VM restore choices; LRS = three copies (verified today for managed disks and for Azure Backup, not on the Storage redundancy page); reserved capacity for Blob Storage (verified today for Azure Files); the raw / cleansed / curated layout of a data lake (design convention, not a product fact).
- **Left out because not verified today:** Azure Cosmos DB for PostgreSQL and PostgreSQL elastic clusters as horizontal-scale relational options; PostgreSQL read-replica count; Azure Files maximum share size; Cosmos DB single-region availability SLA; high-priority rehydration time.
