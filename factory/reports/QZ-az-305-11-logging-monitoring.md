## Out of date or wrong (verified on Learn, Nov. 2026)

- **AMPLS access modes and ingestion: the chapter and the mock question `az305-1a-route-private-ampls` are wrong.** "Design Azure Monitor Private Link configuration" now says: *"Log Analytics ingestion uses resource-specific endpoints, so it doesn't adhere to AMPLS access modes."* To stop ingestion into workspaces outside the AMPLS, you block the public endpoints at the network firewall. The chapter says *Private Only* "prevents data exfiltration" (routing section). The mock question makes *Private Only* the answer to "the VMs must be unable to send logs to a workspace that doesn't belong to Woodgrove". With the current page, that answer isn't enough on its own for ingestion. The question should be fixed (add the firewall, or ask about queries instead of ingestion). q7 avoids access modes.
- **Regional resilience (workspace-design table).** The chapter says only "ingest it into workspaces in different regions". Learn also documents **Log Analytics workspace replication**, a paid feature: you start the switchover yourself, the secondary must be in the same region group, only data ingested after you enable it is replicated, and Auxiliary tables, VM insights and Container insights aren't supported. It isn't in the chapter. Nothing in the quiz depends on it.
- **Alert processing rules.** The chapter only gives suppression as an example. Learn also documents the **Apply action groups** action: it adds action groups to fired alerts without editing the rules. Other facts the chapter could add: scope is within one subscription, rules don't affect Service Health alerts, and a change takes up to 30 minutes to apply. q8 relies on "apply action groups", which the chapter only implies ("changes fired alerts"). Adding one line to the chapter is recommended.
- **Management group diagnostic settings.** The activity log page says a management group setting doesn't capture every subscription-level event (events without the `hierarchy` property). You still need settings on each subscription for full coverage. The chapter mentions "the activity log of a subscription or of a management group" without this nuance.
- Minor: the workspace-design page says a Sentinel workspace gets three months of free retention "instead of 31 days". The retention page says 30 days by default, with some tables at 90 days.
