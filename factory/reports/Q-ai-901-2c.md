## Doubts to review

- **Model line-up moves fast.** The training unit calls GPT-Image-1.5 the latest image model, but the docs page "Azure OpenAI image generation models" now also lists GPT-Image-2 and GPT-Image-2.5 (Sunburst, Flare), and says `dall-e-3` was retired on 2026-03-04. No question asks for "the latest / most advanced" model; questions that compare models restrict the choice to the models named in the options (`imagegen-drafts-and-finals`, `imagegen-edit-product-photo`, `imagegen-family-yn`).
- **Video API surface differs between Learn pages.** The training unit uses `POST /openai/v1/videos` with `model`, `prompt`, `size`, `seconds` and the status `completed`; the docs quickstart also shows `/video/generations/jobs` with `width`, `height`, `n_seconds` and the status `succeeded`. `visionapp-video-rest-yn` and `videogen-async-job-sentences` follow the training unit. The docs say Sora 2 rejects real people and copyrighted characters; `videogen-responsible-ai` words it as "limited by the Responsible AI restrictions", as the unit does.
- **Beyond the learning path, verified on docs pages** (hard questions): the `detail` setting of an image input (`vision-detail-level`), the public-URL requirement for image URLs (`vision-internal-photo-base64`), the `data:image/jpeg;base64,...` data URL (`visionapp-local-image-data-url`), single-shot recognition ending at silence or 15 seconds (`speech-once-vs-continuous`), voice name taking priority over synthesis language (`speech-voice-priority-code-yn`), `speech_recognition_language` and `endpoint_id` (`speech-recognition-language`), the `recognizing` / `recognized` / `session_started` / `canceled` events (`speech-recognized-event`).
- **Image limits were left out**: the Chat Completions how-to says 10 images and 20 MB per image, the Responses API page says 50 images and 512 MB; no question relies on a limit.
- `vision-instructions-requirement` and `imagegen-portal-steps` rely on portal labels from the lab (Instructions, Upload image, Inference tasks > Text to image); their references point to the Learn exercise unit that links to the lab.
- `visionapp-why-base64`: the distractor explanation "the encoded text is larger than the original bytes" is general knowledge of base64, not a sentence of Learn.
- Distractor identifiers that are real but were not each fetched on Learn: `PushAudioInputStream`, `base64.encodebytes`, `response.id`, `response.model`, the `timeout` parameter of the `OpenAI` client, `embeddings.create`.
