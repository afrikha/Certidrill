# SAP-C03 exam profile

- **Format** (SAP-C03 guide, no version or date printed; checked 2026-11-19): 180 minutes, 75 questions (65 scored + 10 unscored), scaled 100–1,000, pass 720, compensatory. Domains: architecture 25 %, security 25 %, cost 15 %, resilience 15 %, operations 20 %. SAP-C02 in use until November 16, 2026; SAP-C03 from November 17, 2026. Our mock scores all 75 (about 2.4 min each).
- **Level**: professional architect for complex organizations (multi-account, multi-Region, hybrid, serverless, AI-integrated). Out of scope: in-depth OS knowledge, model training and data science, incident forensics, application code.
- **Types**: `single` 4 options, 1 correct (78–88 %); `multi` 5 options / 2 correct ("Choose two.") or 6 / 3 ("Choose three."), 12–22 %, all or nothing.
- **Stems**: 5–10 sentences: an organization ("AnyCompany") with several accounts, Regions or business units, its current architecture, constraints (compliance, budget, skills, deadline), then the qualifier ("LEAST operational overhead", "MOST cost-effective", "lowest RPO", "without changing application code") and "Which solution will meet these requirements?". All four options are complete multi-service designs; each distractor fails one stated constraint. Hard = only the qualifier or one buried constraint decides.
- **Traps**: SCPs vs RCPs (resource side, external principals too; not on the management account) vs permissions boundaries; Control Tower, IAM Identity Center, external IDs; Transit Gateway vs peering vs PrivateLink vs VPC Lattice vs Cloud WAN, Direct Connect + VPN, Route 53 Resolver endpoints; backup and restore / pilot light / warm standby / active-active vs RTO/RPO, Elastic Disaster Recovery; Aurora Global Database, DynamoDB global tables, S3 replication; 7 Rs, AWS Transform MGN (ex-Application Migration Service), DMS + SCT, DataSync vs Transfer Family vs Storage Gateway; Savings Plans vs RIs vs Spot, Compute Optimizer, Cost Explorer, CUR; ECS vs EKS; Step Functions Standard vs Express, Lambda durable functions, SQS FIFO, EventBridge cross-account buses.
- **New vs SAP-C02**: domains rebuilt (C02: organizational complexity 26, new solutions 29, continuous improvement 25, migration 20). Added: Bedrock, AgentCore, RAG, guardrails, post-quantum cryptography, data residency, IPv6, lakehouse, service mesh, multi-tenancy, DevSecOps, SLOs, FIS chaos engineering. Removed: IoT, end-user computing, media, Lightsail, SES, Amplify, App Runner, Batch, Proton, Service Catalog.
- **Closures (verified on AWS pages)**: Migration Hub and Application Discovery Service closed to new customers since November 7, 2025 (use AWS Transform). No Snow Family device for new customers (DataSync, Data Transfer Terminal, Outposts). OpsWorks end of life. CodeCommit reopened November 25, 2025. Amazon Q Developer sign-ups closed May 15, 2026 (successor Kiro). Never make a closed service the answer. The guide still writes "Amazon QuickSight" (now Amazon Quick, per A-aws-1).
