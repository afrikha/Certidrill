# AIF-C01 exam profile

- **Format** (guide v1.2, 2026-10-29): 90 minutes, 65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored, not identified), scaled score 100–1,000, pass at 700, compensatory scoring. Domains 20 / 24 / 28 / 14 / 14 %. The mock exam scores all 65.
- **Level**: foundational, up to 6 months of AI/ML exposure on AWS; uses AI/ML solutions, does not build them (no coding, tuning or pipeline building).
- **Types** (all scored all or nothing): `single`, 4 options (72–82 %); `multi`, 5 options with 2 correct ("Choose two."), rarely 6 with 3 (10–16 %); `ordering`, a list of 3–5 responses for one task, to select and put in the right order (ML pipeline stages, FM lifecycle) (3–7 %); `dragdrop` = AWS **matching**: 3–7 prompts to match with a list of responses (4–8 %). Case-study scenarios become standalone questions.
- **Stems**: a short business scenario (1–4 sentences, "A retail company") and one concept, technique, metric or service to identify ("Which solution will meet these requirements?"), often with a qualifier ("MOST cost-effective", "LEAST operational overhead"). Distractors from the same family.
- **Classic traps**: supervised vs unsupervised vs reinforcement learning; classification vs regression vs clustering; accuracy / precision / recall / F1 / AUC (ML) vs ROUGE (summaries) vs BLEU (translation) vs BERTScore (semantic similarity) vs LLM-as-a-judge; prompt engineering vs RAG vs fine-tuning vs continued pre-training vs distillation; temperature, top-p, top-k, maximum length; tokens, context window, token-based pricing vs Provisioned Throughput; overfitting vs underfitting; Bedrock (FMs by API) vs SageMaker AI (build, train, host) vs JumpStart (pretrained models) vs Quick vs Kiro; Bedrock Guardrails vs Knowledge Bases vs AgentCore vs Prompt Management vs Model Evaluation; Clarify (bias) vs Model Monitor (drift) vs Model Cards (documentation); hallucination vs grounding, prompt injection vs jailbreaking vs poisoning; Config vs CloudTrail vs Artifact vs Inspector vs Macie.
- **Recent names** (verified): Amazon SageMaker AI (renamed 2024; "Amazon SageMaker" = unified data and AI platform); Amazon Nova FMs on Bedrock; Amazon Quick is the guide's name, AWS documentation now says **AWS Quick** (from Amazon QuickSight); Kiro replaces Amazon Q Developer in IDEs; Bedrock Agents is now Bedrock Agents Classic, AgentCore is the successor.
