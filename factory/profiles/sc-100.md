# SC-100 exam profile

- 100 minutes, 40–60 questions, pass at 700/1000. Microsoft Learn is reachable during the exam. Plan one case study (4–6 questions: existing environment, requirements, constraints) and one "does the solution meet the goal" series of 3.
- **Level**: expert, architect. A pure **design** exam: recommend, evaluate, specify. No command, no code, no KQL, no portal click path. The candidate must know what each Microsoft security product does, where it fits, and which framework guides a decision.
- **Stems**: 4–8 sentences. A hybrid or multicloud organization (Azure, AWS, GCP, on-premises AD DS, Microsoft 365), business and compliance requirements, then "What should you recommend?", "What should you include in the recommendation?", "Which two solutions meet the requirements?". One constraint decides: minimize cost or administrative effort, Zero Trust, least privilege, multicloud coverage.
- **Formats**: single choice (40–50 %), "select two" (10–15 %), Yes/No statements (10–15 %), drop-down "to meet X, recommend Y" (10–15 %), drag and drop requirement-to-solution (5–10 %); ordering and hot area are rare.
- **Traps to cover**: MCRA vs MCSB vs CAF vs WAF vs Zero Trust adoption framework; Defender for Cloud posture management vs workload protection; Defender for Cloud secure score vs Microsoft Secure Score; PIM vs entitlement management vs access reviews; Sentinel vs Defender XDR; Microsoft Entra Internet Access vs Private Access; Azure Policy vs Defender for Cloud regulatory compliance vs Purview; Defender for IoT vs Defender EASM; workload identity vs agent identity.
- **Out of scope**: step-by-step implementation, configuration values, prices.
- Difficulty: 30 % easy, 50 % medium, 20 % hard (hard = several requirements with one constraint that eliminates the obvious answer).
- The October 21, 2026 guide adds recent topics (Microsoft Entra Agent ID, secure AI adoption, Microsoft 365 Copilot controls, Security Exposure Management): fetch every fact from Microsoft Learn.
